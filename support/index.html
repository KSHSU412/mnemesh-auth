回報問題

請提供 App 版本、iOS／watchOS 版本、操作步驟、預期與實際結果。可附上你願意分享的截圖；請先遮住姓名、信箱與不必要的健康資料。不要寄送密碼、驗證碼、登入連結或完整資料庫。TestFlight 也可以直接傳送測試回饋。

資料無法開啟

請勿卸載或清空 App。先解鎖裝置、確認可用空間，再按「重新嘗試開啟」。若仍失敗，聯絡支援；沒有成功同步的紀錄可能只在裝置內。復原畫面不代表已經修復損壞的資料庫。

同步、健康與 AI

「手機已保存」「健康已保存」「雲端待同步」是不同狀態。未登入時可只在本機記錄；登入後同步該帳號資料。Apple 健康與 AI 連接都需另外授權。

刪除帳號

在 MuMesh「設定 → 刪除帳號」操作，不需要寄信才能發起。請先匯出想保留的紀錄。刪除需連網確認，未完成會保留重試狀態。Apple 健康、照片、行事曆、AI 對話與其他裝置的副本需另外管理。若曾使用 Apple 登入，完成刪除後也請到 Apple 帳號的「使用 Apple 登入」移除 MuMesh。

English

Include your app and OS versions, reproduction steps, expected result and actual result. Screenshots are optional; redact unnecessary personal and health information. Never send passwords, verification codes, login links or a full database. TestFlight also offers a feedback channel.

If your data cannot open, do not uninstall or reset the app. Unlock the device, check available storage and retry. Contact support if it still fails. Unsynced workouts may exist only on your device; the recovery screen does not imply that corruption has been repaired.

Local save, Apple Health save and cloud sync are independent. Account sign-in is optional for local logging; Health and AI connections require separate authorization.

To initiate account deletion, use Settings → Delete account in MuMesh. Export anything you need first. The app retains a retry state until deletion is confirmed. Manage Apple Health, photos, calendar events, AI conversations and other-device copies separately. If you used Sign in with Apple, also remove MuMesh from your Apple Account's Sign in with Apple settings afterward.
