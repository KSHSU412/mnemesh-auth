MuMesh 隱私政策／Privacy Policy

MuMesh 原名 MNEMESH。此次更名不改變帳號、資料用途或隱私原則；既有 Apple 授權與 AI 連接可能仍顯示舊名稱。Formerly MNEMESH. This rename does not change accounts, data use or privacy practices; existing Apple grants and AI connections may retain the former name.

繁體中文

MuMesh 是 iPhone 與 Apple Watch 的重訓紀錄工具。你可以不建立帳號，僅在裝置上記錄；登入後，屬於該帳號的訓練及計畫會同步至 Supabase。原先純本機紀錄不會因登入自動歸入帳號。MuMesh 沒有廣告、沒有第三方分析工具，也不販售任何資料。

使用哪些資料

  • 帳號：電子郵件、登入服務提供的姓名、使用者識別碼與登入憑證，用於驗證及同步。憑證不放入公開分享卡片。
  • 訓練與計畫：動作、重量、次數或秒數、時間、備註、來源及計畫內容，用於紀錄、同步、查詢和你授權的 AI 工具操作。
  • Apple 健康：只有取得系統授權後才讀取心率／活動熱量或寫入訓練。心率與健康摘要用於裝置上的顯示，不經訓練同步上傳到 Supabase。訓練時間與組數等健身紀錄在登入後會同步。
  • 手錶動作資料：開啟「採集」後，Apple Watch 會在訓練時記錄六軸動作（加速度與角速度）和收到的心率，結束後存到你的 iPhone。它只存在你的手錶與 iPhone，不會自動上傳；你可以隨時關閉採集或刪除已存的資料。
  • 同步紀錄與問題報告：為了排除手機與手錶同步問題，App 在裝置上保留有限的操作紀錄（時間、狀態與錯誤類型，不含動作名稱、重量、次數、心率或感測資料）。只有你主動分享時才會離開裝置。
  • 連接紀錄與設定：AI 工具名稱、操作時間與結果、行事曆匯出偏好，用於功能運作及排錯。
  • 支援：只有你主動聯絡時提供的訊息或附件。請勿寄送密碼、登入連結或不必要的健康資料。

協助改善自動記組（自願）

MuMesh 正在開發「選好動作後自動記組」。你可以在 App「設定 → 帳號與資料 → 協助改善自動記組」自願參加，參加前會看到完整說明並需按下同意；App 會在你的裝置上記錄同意與退出的時間和說明版本。

  • 提供內容：只有你每次自己選擇的那場訓練，包含手錶六軸動作資料、採集期間收到的心率、每組的動作、重量、次數與時間，以及那場的同步紀錄。不包含電子郵件、姓名、帳號識別碼或備註。
  • 提供方式：你用系統分享（例如 AirDrop、LINE 或 Email）傳給開發者；App 不會自動上傳。經由 LINE 或 Email 傳送時，副本也會經過該服務。
  • 用途：只用於開發與驗證自動記組與漏記助手。不販售、不外流、不用於廣告，也不提供給其他公司。
  • 保存：存放在開發者自己的電腦。該項開發結束時（包含停止這個計畫），會刪除所有收到的資料；你也可以隨時要求刪除。刪除後，已用這些資料訓練出的模型不含可辨識的個人資料，可能會繼續使用。
  • 風險：動作與心率屬於和健康相關的個人資料，可能看出你的運動習慣與時間。
  • 退出與刪除：可隨時在 App 內停止參加；已傳出的資料請來信要求刪除。參加者須年滿 18 歲。

開發者作為服務營運者，只會為了服務運作、排除問題、安全或依你的要求處理雲端紀錄；不會把你同步到雲端的訓練紀錄用於開發或訓練自動記組模型，除非你另外明確同意。

分享與第三方

MuMesh 不販售個人或健康資料，不加入廣告追蹤，也不將 Apple 健康資料用於廣告。Supabase 處理登入、雲端儲存與服務運作；Apple 處理 Apple 登入、健康與 TestFlight；Google 在你選用 Google 登入時處理登入。基礎服務可能保留安全、連線與故障日誌。

連接 ChatGPT 或其他 AI 服務是選用功能。授權的服務可透過工具讀取訓練／計畫並寫入你要求的內容；回傳資料會交給該服務，受其自身隱私政策及帳號設定管理。不要授權不信任的服務。取消連接不會移除已存在 AI 對話中的副本。你也可以把已預覽的診斷上傳，供已授權的 AI 連接在 24 小時或 7 天內讀取；它不含心率或原始感測資料，可隨時停止分享。

匯出 JSON／CSV、照片分享卡片、行事曆事件、感測資料副本與分享給他人的檔案，只有你選擇操作時才交給所選目的地；其後的保存由目的地控制。原始訓練備註可能包含你自行輸入的敏感內容。

保存、刪除與權利

帳號與雲端紀錄保留至你刪除帳號或依服務維護需要處理。到 App「設定 → 刪除帳號」可永久移除帳號、相關雲端紀錄與 AI 授權。必須連網取得刪除確認；失敗會顯示並保留重試狀態，不會假稱成功。本機清理限定該帳號，其他帳號與純本機紀錄不會被一起刪除。連線的配對手錶會收到清理通知；其他裝置可能仍有離線副本，需另外管理。服務供應商的安全日誌與既有備份可能依其保存週期延後到期；不承諾所有備份即時抹除。

Apple 健康、已匯出的檔案、照片、行事曆、傳給他人的副本及 AI 對話中的副本需在各服務另外刪除。若曾使用 Apple 登入，目前刪除完成後會指引你在 Apple 帳號設定撤銷 MuMesh 的 Apple 登入授權；這與 MuMesh 帳號刪除是不同步驟。

資料庫無法開啟時，App 暫停記錄及同步、不自動清空資料；請先聯絡支援，不要卸載，以免失去未同步紀錄。可隨時在系統設定調整健康授權，或在 AI 服務中取消 MuMesh 連接。

TestFlight 測試版可能包含尚未正式推出的功能；Apple 會依其 TestFlight 條款處理測試者資訊與當機報告。資料可能由境外服務處理。本政策更新會於此頁標示日期。資料存取、刪除或隱私問題請聯絡：kshsu.dev@gmail.com。

English

MuMesh is a strength-training logger for iPhone and Apple Watch. You may log locally without an account. Signing in enables Supabase sync for that account's workouts and plans; previously local-only records are not automatically assigned to your account. MuMesh has no ads and no third-party analytics, and never sells data.

We process your email, name supplied by a sign-in provider, user identifier and authentication credentials for account access; workout exercises, weights, reps or seconds, timestamps, notes and plans for logging and sync; and connector operation records and calendar export preferences for app functionality and troubleshooting.

Apple Health access is optional and system-authorized. Heart rate and Health summaries are displayed on-device and are not uploaded through MuMesh's Supabase workout sync. Fitness records such as workout times and sets do sync when signed in.

Watch motion data: with capture turned on, Apple Watch records accelerometer and gyroscope data and received heart rate during workouts and saves it to your iPhone afterwards. It stays on your Watch and iPhone and is never uploaded automatically; you can turn capture off or delete saved data at any time. Sync logs and problem reports keep limited on-device records (times, states and error types, without exercise names, weights, reps, heart rate or sensor data) to troubleshoot phone–Watch sync; they leave your device only when you share them. Support receives only messages and attachments you choose to send.

Help improve automatic logging (voluntary)

MuMesh is building automatic set logging. You may join in Settings → Account & data → Help improve automatic logging after reading the full disclosure and agreeing; the app records when you agreed or left, and the disclosure version, on your device. You provide only the workouts you choose each time: Watch motion data, heart rate received during capture, each set's exercise, weight, reps and time, and that workout's sync log — no email, name, account identifier or notes. You send it yourself through the system share sheet (for example AirDrop, LINE or email); nothing is uploaded automatically, and LINE or email also carry a copy. It is used only to build and check automatic set logging and the missed-set assistant, never sold, never leaked, never used for ads and never given to other companies. It is kept on the developer's own computer; when that work ends (including if the project is stopped), all received data is deleted, and you can ask for deletion at any time. Models already trained on it contain no identifiable personal data and may continue to be used after deletion. Motion and heart rate are personal, health-related data and can show your exercise habits and times. You can stop at any time in the app and email to delete what you sent. Participants must be 18 or older.

As the service operator, the developer handles cloud records only to run and secure the service, troubleshoot, or at your request, and does not use your synced workout records to build or train automatic logging models unless you explicitly agree.

MuMesh does not sell personal or health data, add advertising tracking, or use Apple Health data for advertising. Supabase operates authentication and cloud storage. Apple operates Sign in with Apple, Health and TestFlight; Google handles Google sign-in when selected. Infrastructure providers may retain operational and security logs.

AI connections are optional. Authorized AI services can read workouts/plans and write requested records using tools. Data returned to an AI service is governed by that service's own policies and account settings. Disconnecting does not erase existing AI conversations. You may also upload a previewed diagnostic for an authorized AI connection to read for 24 hours or 7 days; it excludes heart rate and raw sensor data and can be withdrawn at any time. Files, photos, calendar exports and sensor data copies are shared only when you choose a destination; copies are then controlled by that destination.

Use Settings → Delete account to delete your MuMesh account, associated live cloud records and AI authorizations. Deletion requires network confirmation and retains a retry state on failure. Local cleanup applies only to that account, not other accounts or local-only records. A paired Watch receives cleanup instructions when connected; other devices may retain offline copies. Provider backups and security logs can expire on their own retention schedules rather than immediately. Manage Apple Health records, exports, photos, calendar entries, copies sent to others and AI conversations separately. If you used Sign in with Apple, the current deletion flow also directs you to revoke MuMesh in your Apple Account settings.

If the local database cannot open, MuMesh pauses recording/sync and does not automatically erase it. Contact support before uninstalling, which can lose unsynced workouts. You can change Health permissions in system settings or disconnect MuMesh in your AI service.

TestFlight builds may include features that are not yet released; Apple handles tester information and crash reports under its TestFlight terms. Service providers may process data outside your country. This page will show the date of policy updates. For privacy, access or deletion questions: kshsu.dev@gmail.com.
