MNEMESH 隱私政策／Privacy Policy

繁體中文

MNEMESH 是重訓紀錄工具。你可以不建立帳號，僅在裝置上記錄；登入後，屬於該帳號的訓練及計畫會同步至 Supabase。原先純本機紀錄不會因登入自動歸入帳號。

使用哪些資料

  • 帳號：電子郵件、登入服務提供的姓名、使用者識別碼與登入憑證，用於驗證及同步。憑證不放入公開分享卡片。
  • 訓練與計畫：動作、重量、次數或秒數、時間、備註、來源及計畫內容，用於紀錄、同步、查詢和你授權的 AI 工具操作。
  • 連接紀錄與設定：AI 工具名稱、操作時間與結果、行事曆匯出偏好，用於功能運作及排錯。
  • Apple 健康：只有取得系統授權後才讀取心率／活動熱量或寫入訓練。原始心率序列與健康摘要目前用於裝置上的顯示，不由 MNEMESH 的訓練同步介面上傳到 Supabase。訓練時間與組數等健身紀錄在登入後會同步。
  • 支援：只有你主動聯絡時提供的訊息或附件。請勿寄送密碼、登入連結或不必要的健康資料。

分享與第三方

MNEMESH 不販售個人或健康資料，不加入廣告追蹤，也不將 Apple 健康資料用於廣告。Supabase 處理登入、雲端儲存與服務運作；Apple 處理 Apple 登入、健康與 TestFlight；Google 在你選用 Google 登入時處理登入。基礎服務可能保留安全、連線與故障日誌。

連接 ChatGPT 或其他 AI 服務是選用功能。授權的服務可透過工具讀取訓練／計畫並寫入你要求的內容；回傳資料會交給該服務，受其自身隱私政策及帳號設定管理。不要授權不信任的服務。取消連接不會移除已存在 AI 對話中的副本。

匯出 JSON／CSV、照片分享卡片、行事曆事件與分享給他人的副本，只有你選擇操作時才交給所選目的地；其後的保存由目的地控制。原始訓練備註可能包含你自行輸入的敏感內容。

保存、刪除與權利

帳號與雲端紀錄保留至你刪除帳號或依服務維護需要處理。到 App「設定 → 刪除帳號」可永久移除帳號、相關雲端紀錄與 AI 授權。必須連網取得刪除確認；失敗會顯示並保留重試狀態，不會假稱成功。本機清理限定該帳號，其他帳號與純本機紀錄不會被一起刪除。連線的配對手錶會收到清理通知；其他裝置可能仍有離線副本，需另外管理。服務供應商的安全日誌與既有備份可能依其保存週期延後到期；不承諾所有備份即時抹除。

Apple 健康、已匯出的檔案、照片、行事曆及 AI 對話中的副本需在各服務另外刪除。若曾使用 Apple 登入，目前刪除完成後會指引你在 Apple 帳號設定撤銷 MNEMESH 的 Apple 登入授權；這與 MNEMESH 帳號刪除是不同步驟。

資料庫無法開啟時，App 暫停記錄及同步、不自動清空資料；請先聯絡支援，不要卸載，以免失去未同步紀錄。可隨時在系統設定調整健康授權，或在 AI 服務中取消 MNEMESH 連接。

資料可能由境外服務處理。本政策更新會於此頁標示日期。資料存取、刪除或隱私問題請聯絡：kshsu.dev@gmail.com。

English

MNEMESH is a strength-training logger. You may log locally without an account. Signing in enables Supabase sync for that account's workouts and plans; previously local-only records are not automatically assigned to your account.

We process your email, name supplied by a sign-in provider, user identifier and authentication credentials for account access; workout exercises, weights, reps or seconds, timestamps, notes and plans for logging and sync; and connector operation records and calendar export preferences for app functionality and troubleshooting.

Apple Health access is optional and system-authorized. Raw heart-rate samples and Health summaries are currently displayed on-device and are not uploaded through MNEMESH's Supabase workout sync. Fitness records such as workout times and sets do sync when signed in. Support receives only messages and attachments you choose to send.

MNEMESH does not sell personal or health data, add advertising tracking, or use Apple Health data for advertising. Supabase operates authentication and cloud storage. Apple operates Sign in with Apple, Health and TestFlight; Google handles Google sign-in when selected. Infrastructure providers may retain operational and security logs.

AI connections are optional. Authorized AI services can read workouts/plans and write requested records using tools. Data returned to an AI service is governed by that service's own policies and account settings. Disconnecting does not erase existing AI conversations. Files, photos and calendar exports are shared only when you choose an export destination; copies are then controlled by that destination.

Use Settings → Delete account to delete your MNEMESH account, associated live cloud records and AI authorizations. Deletion requires network confirmation and retains a retry state on failure. Local cleanup applies only to that account, not other accounts or local-only records. A paired Watch receives cleanup instructions when connected; other devices may retain offline copies. Provider backups and security logs can expire on their own retention schedules rather than immediately. Manage Apple Health records, exports, photos, calendar entries and AI conversations separately. If you used Sign in with Apple, the current deletion flow also directs you to revoke MNEMESH in your Apple Account settings.

If the local database cannot open, MNEMESH pauses recording/sync and does not automatically erase it. Contact support before uninstalling, which can lose unsynced workouts. You can change Health permissions in system settings or disconnect MNEMESH in your AI service.

Service providers may process data outside your country. This page will show the date of policy updates. For privacy, access or deletion questions: kshsu.dev@gmail.com.
