MuMesh 隱私政策／Privacy Policy

MuMesh 原名 MNEMESH。既有的 Apple 授權與 AI 連接可能仍顯示舊名稱。Formerly MNEMESH. Existing Apple grants and AI connections may still show the former name.

繁體中文

MuMesh 是 iPhone 與 Apple Watch 的重訓紀錄工具。你可以不建立帳號，只在裝置上記錄；登入後，屬於該帳號的訓練與計畫會同步到雲端。原本只存在本機的紀錄不會因為登入就自動歸入帳號。MuMesh 沒有廣告、沒有第三方分析工具，也不販售任何資料。

誰在處理你的資料

MuMesh 由個人開發者許凱翔（Kai-Siang Hsu）在台灣開發與營運。隱私相關的問題與請求請寄到 kshsu.dev@gmail.com。

使用哪些資料、用來做什麼

  • 帳號：電子郵件、登入服務提供的姓名、使用者識別碼與登入憑證，用於登入與同步。
  • 訓練與計畫：動作、重量、次數或秒數、時間、備註、來源與計畫內容，用於紀錄、同步、查詢，以及你授權的 AI 工具操作。
  • Apple 健康：取得系統授權後才會讀取心率與活動熱量，或寫入訓練。心率與健康摘要只在裝置上顯示，不會經由訓練同步上傳到雲端。訓練時間與組數等紀錄在登入後會同步。
  • 手錶動作資料：開啟「採集」後，Apple Watch 會在訓練時記錄六軸動作（加速度與角速度）和收到的心率，結束後存到你的 iPhone。它只存在你的手錶與 iPhone，不會自動上傳；你可以隨時關閉採集或刪除已存的資料。
  • 同步紀錄與問題報告：為了排除手機與手錶的同步問題，App 在裝置上保留有限的操作紀錄（時間、狀態與錯誤類型，不含動作名稱、重量、次數、心率或感測資料）。只有你主動分享時才會離開裝置。
  • AI 連接紀錄與設定：AI 工具名稱、操作時間與結果，以及行事曆匯出偏好，用於功能運作與排除問題。
  • 場次計數：登入後，雲端會記下你的帳號有哪幾場訓練記過組（訓練的識別碼、開始時間、記下第一組的時間、來源裝置或工具，以及是否為測試訓練），用於之後計算方案額度。目前只計算，不收費也不限制。刪除單場訓練不會刪除這筆計數。
  • 支援：只有你主動聯絡時提供的訊息與附件。請不要寄送密碼、登入連結或不必要的健康資料。

帳號、訓練、計畫、AI 連接紀錄與場次計數，是為了提供你使用的服務而處理。資料貢獻另外以你的同意為依據，見下一節。

協助改善自動記組（自願）

MuMesh 正在開發「選好動作後自動記組」。你可以在 App「設定 → 帳號與資料 → 協助改善自動記組」自願參加。參加前會看到完整說明並需要按下同意；App 會記錄你同意與退出的時間和說明版本。

  • 提供內容：只有你每次自己選擇的那場訓練，包含手錶六軸動作資料、每組的動作、重量、次數與時間，以及那場的同步紀錄。檔案會附上你同意的說明版本與同意時間。不包含心率、電子郵件、姓名、帳號識別碼或備註。
  • 提供方式：你用系統分享（例如 AirDrop、LINE 或 Email）傳給開發者；App 不會自動上傳。經由 LINE 或 Email 傳送時，副本也會經過該服務。
  • 用途：只用於開發與驗證自動記組和漏記助手。不販售、不用於廣告，也不交給其他公司或外部 AI 服務處理。
  • 保存：保存在開發者自己的電腦上，只有開發者本人使用。原始資料從收到起最長保存 24 個月；計畫提早停止，或你來信要求時，會更早刪除。如果資料已經用來訓練模型，刪除原始資料後，已訓練的模型可能會繼續使用。為了之後能證明你同意過，同意紀錄（說明版本、同意時間、是哪一場訓練）會在原始資料刪除後再保留 5 年，其中不含動作資料。
  • 風險：動作資料是個人資料，可能看出你的運動習慣與時間。開發者會用合理的方式保護，但沒有任何方式能保證絕對不會外洩。
  • 退出與刪除：可以隨時在 App 內停止參加；已傳出的資料請來信要求刪除。參加者須年滿 18 歲。

開發者作為服務營運者，只會為了服務運作、排除問題、安全或依你的要求處理雲端紀錄；不會把你同步到雲端的訓練紀錄用於開發或訓練自動記組模型，除非你另外明確同意。

資料放在哪裡、交給誰

  • 雲端資料由 Supabase 代為儲存與處理，資料庫位於新加坡。Supabase 也處理登入。
  • Apple 處理 Apple 登入、Apple 健康與 TestFlight；Google 在你選用 Google 登入時處理登入。這些服務可能在其他國家處理資料，並保留安全、連線與故障日誌。
  • MuMesh 不販售個人或健康資料，不加入廣告追蹤，也不將 Apple 健康資料用於廣告。
  • 連接 ChatGPT 或其他 AI 服務是選用功能。你授權的服務可以透過工具讀取訓練與計畫，並寫入你要求的內容；交給該服務的資料由它自己的隱私政策與帳號設定管理。不要授權你不信任的服務。取消連接不會移除已經存在 AI 對話裡的副本。你也可以把預覽過的診斷報告上傳，讓已授權的 AI 連接在 24 小時或 7 天內讀取；它不含心率或原始感測資料，可以隨時停止分享。
  • 匯出的 JSON 或 CSV、照片分享卡片、行事曆事件、感測資料副本與傳給他人的檔案，只有在你操作時才會交給你選的目的地，之後由該目的地保存。訓練備註可能包含你自己輸入的敏感內容。

刪除帳號

在 App「設定 → 刪除帳號」可以永久移除帳號、相關的雲端紀錄、場次計數與 AI 授權。

  • 刪除需要連網取得確認；失敗時會顯示並保留重試狀態，不會假稱成功。
  • 用 Apple 登入的帳號，刪除時會請你再用 Apple 登入一次，用來一併撤銷 MuMesh 的 Apple 登入授權。撤銷失敗時你仍然可以刪除帳號，再自己到 iPhone「設定 → 你的姓名 → 使用 Apple 登入」移除。
  • 本機清理只針對這個帳號，其他帳號與純本機紀錄不會被一起刪除。配對的手錶連線後會收到清理通知；其他裝置上的離線副本需要另外處理。
  • Apple 健康、已匯出的檔案、照片、行事曆、傳給他人的副本，以及 AI 對話裡的副本，需要在各自的服務裡另外刪除。

  • 要求補充或更正。

你也可以隨時在系統設定調整健康與動作的授權，或在 AI 服務裡取消 MuMesh 的連接。

不提供資料會怎樣

不登入也可以在裝置上記錄訓練，只是不會同步，也不能使用 AI 連接。不授權 Apple 健康，App 不會顯示心率，也不會把訓練寫入健康。不參加資料貢獻，不影響任何功能。

未滿 18 歲

MuMesh 不是為兒童設計的。未滿 18 歲請先取得法定代理人同意再建立帳號。資料貢獻只開放給年滿 18 歲的使用者。

安全與資料外洩

雲端資料以帳號為單位做存取控管，傳輸過程加密。沒有任何方式能保證絕對安全；如果發生影響你個人資料的外洩，查明後會盡快用 App 或你的帳號信箱通知你，說明發生了什麼以及已經採取的處理。

English

MuMesh is a strength-training logger for iPhone and Apple Watch. You can log on your device without an account. After you sign in, that account's workouts and plans sync to the cloud; records that were only on the device are not moved into the account automatically. MuMesh has no ads and no third-party analytics, and never sells data.

Who handles your data

MuMesh is built and run by an individual developer, Kai-Siang Hsu (許凱翔), in Taiwan. Send privacy questions and requests to kshsu.dev@gmail.com.

What data is used, and for what

  • Account: your email, the name supplied by your sign-in provider, a user identifier and sign-in credentials, for signing in and syncing.
  • Workouts and plans: exercises, weights, reps or seconds, times, notes, sources and plan contents, for logging, syncing, lookups, and actions by AI tools you authorize.
  • Apple Health: only with system permission does the app read heart rate and active energy, or write workouts. Heart rate and Health summaries are shown on the device and are not uploaded through workout sync. Records such as workout times and sets do sync when you are signed in.
  • Watch motion data: with capture turned on, Apple Watch records accelerometer and gyroscope data and received heart rate during workouts and saves it to your iPhone afterwards. It stays on your Watch and iPhone and is never uploaded automatically; you can turn capture off or delete saved data at any time.
  • Sync logs and problem reports: to troubleshoot phone–Watch sync, the app keeps limited records on the device (times, states and error types, without exercise names, weights, reps, heart rate or sensor data). They leave the device only when you share them.
  • AI connection records and settings: the AI tool's name, when it acted and the result, and calendar export preferences, to run the feature and troubleshoot.
  • Session count: when you are signed in, the cloud notes which of your workouts had a set logged (the workout's identifier, its start time, when its first set was logged, the device or tool it came from, and whether it is a test workout) to work out plan allowances later. For now it only counts: nothing is charged or limited. Deleting a workout does not remove its count entry.
  • Support: only the messages and attachments you choose to send. Please do not send passwords, sign-in links or health data that is not needed.

Account, workout, plan, AI connection and session count data are handled to provide the service you use. Data contribution rests on your consent instead; see the next section.

Help improve automatic logging (voluntary)

MuMesh is building automatic set logging after you choose an exercise. You may join in Settings → Account & data → Help improve automatic logging. You see the full disclosure and must agree first; the app records when you agreed or left, and the disclosure version.

  • What you provide: only the workouts you choose each time, with the Watch motion data, each set's exercise, weight, reps and time, and that workout's sync log. The file carries the disclosure version you agreed to and when you agreed. No heart rate, email, name, account identifier or notes.
  • How: you send it to the developer yourself through the system share sheet (for example AirDrop, LINE or email); the app uploads nothing automatically. When sent by LINE or email, a copy also passes through that service.
  • Use: only to build and check automatic set logging and the missed-set assistant. Not sold, not used for ads, and not given to other companies or outside AI services to process.
  • Keeping: kept on the developer's own computer and used only by the developer. Raw data is kept for at most 24 months from receipt, and deleted earlier if the project stops or you ask by email. If your data has already been used to train a model, that model may continue to be used after the raw data is deleted. To be able to show later that you agreed, the consent record (disclosure version, time of consent, which workout) is kept for 5 more years after the raw data is deleted; it contains no motion data.
  • Risk: motion data is personal data and can show your exercise habits and times. The developer protects it with reasonable care, but no method can guarantee it will never be exposed.
  • Leaving and deleting: you can stop at any time in the app; email to delete what you already sent. Participants must be 18 or older.

As the service operator, the developer handles cloud records only to run and secure the service, troubleshoot, or at your request, and does not use your synced workout records to build or train automatic logging models unless you explicitly agree.

Where data is kept and who receives it

  • Cloud data is stored and processed by Supabase on the developer's behalf; the database is in Singapore. Supabase also handles sign-in.
  • Apple handles Sign in with Apple, Apple Health and TestFlight; Google handles sign-in when you choose Google. These services may process data in other countries and keep security, connection and fault logs.
  • MuMesh does not sell personal or health data, add advertising tracking, or use Apple Health data for advertising.
  • Connecting ChatGPT or another AI service is optional. A service you authorize can read workouts and plans through tools and write what you ask for; data handed to that service is governed by its own privacy policy and account settings. Do not authorize a service you do not trust. Disconnecting does not remove copies already in AI conversations. You may also upload a previewed diagnostic report for an authorized AI connection to read for 24 hours or 7 days; it has no heart rate or raw sensor data, and you can stop sharing at any time.
  • Exported JSON or CSV, photo share cards, calendar events, sensor data copies and files sent to others go to the destination you choose only when you act, and are then kept by that destination. Workout notes may contain sensitive content you typed yourself.

Deleting your account

Settings → Delete account in the app permanently removes your account, its cloud records, its session count and its AI authorizations.

  • Deletion needs a network confirmation; a failure is shown and a retry state is kept, and the app never claims success it did not get.
  • If the account uses Sign in with Apple, deletion asks you to sign in with Apple once more, so that MuMesh's Sign in with Apple access is revoked with the account. If the revoke fails you can still delete the account, and then remove MuMesh yourself in iPhone Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple.
  • Local cleanup applies only to this account; other accounts and local-only records are not deleted with it. A paired Watch receives a cleanup notice when it connects; offline copies on other devices need to be handled separately.
  • Apple Health, exported files, photos, calendar entries, copies sent to others and copies in AI conversations need to be deleted in each of those services.

Your rights

For your personal data that MuMesh handles, you may:

  • ask what is held or view it, or ask for a copy. Settings in the app can also export your workout records directly;
  • ask for it to be completed or corrected;
  • ask for collection, processing or use to stop;
  • ask for it to be deleted.

Email kshsu.dev@gmail.com. To protect you, the request is first confirmed to come from the account holder. Requests to view or copy are answered within 15 days; requests to correct, stop or delete within 30 days; if more time is needed you are told why first. These requests are currently free.

You can also change Health and motion permissions in system settings at any time, or disconnect MuMesh in your AI service.

If you do not provide data

Without signing in you can still log workouts on your device; they do not sync and AI connections are not available. Without Apple Health permission the app shows no heart rate and writes no workouts to Health. Not joining data contribution affects no feature.

Under 18

MuMesh is not designed for children. If you are under 18, get your legal guardian's consent before creating an account. Data contribution is open only to users aged 18 or older.

Security and data breaches

Cloud data is access-controlled per account and encrypted in transit. No method can guarantee complete security; if a breach affects your personal data, you will be told as soon as it is understood, in the app or at your account email, with what happened and what has been done.

Other

  • TestFlight builds may include features that are not yet released; Apple handles tester information and crash reports under its TestFlight terms.
  • When this policy changes, this page shows the version and date; a change of purpose or a new kind of data is also announced in the app.
